SkinProof measurement badge: specification for a shop's reviewer

A badge describes how a measured score changed between a brand's own before and after photos, compared with measurement noise. It is not a statement about a product. Fictional example, not legal advice.

Three states

StateWhenAllowed text
Beyond noiseat least one score changed by more than twice the noise measured from the two retakes"N of 12 scores moved beyond measurement noise"
No clear changeevery change is inside the noise bar"No clear change beyond measurement noise"
Cannot judgethe two retakes of a pair disagree too much, or too few scores"Retakes disagree; cannot judge"

Wording rules

UseDo not use
Describe scores and measurement ("moved", "beyond noise", "no clear change")Any outcome claim about skin or about the product's effect
State that retakes and framing affect scoresAny ingredient name next to a result
Show the photos exactly as supplied by the brandRetouched or substituted photos (the badge cannot detect them)

The banned-word lists are the regular expressions in test/claims.test.js; the build fails if user-facing text matches them.

What a reviewer should ask the brand

  1. Were baseline and follow-up photos taken in the same light, distance and pose, two photos each?
  2. Is the interval stated (two weeks or more)?
  3. Were the photos unretouched?
